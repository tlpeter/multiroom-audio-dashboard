# Multiroom Audio Dashboard 2.2.0-beta.5

**See what is playing.** The now-playing area shows the album art from Home Assistant and a **Zoek videoclip op YouTube** button. If the main player is a group without track information, the card now takes title, artist and album from the speaker that is actually playing. (#25)

**Optional online info.** Turn on **Online info over het nummer** under *Kleuren & uiterlijk* to look up the track at MusicBrainz, Wikidata and Wikipedia: release year, a short text about the artist (Dutch, English fallback) and a Cover Art Archive cover when Home Assistant has none. It is off by default; with it off nothing is sent to these services. Requests are rate limited, cached and validated, and the sources are credited. Lyrics are not shown (copyright). The YouTube button only opens a search when you click it.

Tested automatically with synthetic Home Assistant states and mocked online services (unit and browser tests, desktop and mobile). Not yet verified on a live Home Assistant with Music Assistant.

Update through HACS and reload the browser. Existing configurations remain compatible.
