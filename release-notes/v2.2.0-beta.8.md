# Multiroom Audio Dashboard 2.2.0-beta.8

**Search your music.** The card has a new **Muziek zoeken** section. Type an artist, album, track or playlist, press Enter, and pick a result: **▶ Afspelen** plays it now, **Volgende** puts it right after the current track and **+ Wachtrij** adds it to the end of the queue. Filters narrow the results to tracks, albums, artists or playlists. (#38)

- Works with players that support searching, in practice **Music Assistant** players. Choose a Music Assistant player as main player (or add one as speaker) via **⚙ Speakers beheren**. ESPHome/Sendspin players cannot search themselves.
- Uses the standard Home Assistant actions `media_player.search_media` and `media_player.play_media`.
- Nothing is searched or played until you click. Results are checked, limited to 40 and can only be played on the player that was searched.

Tested automatically with synthetic Home Assistant data (unit and browser tests, desktop and mobile). Not yet verified with a live Music Assistant server.

Update through the Home Assistant Marketplace and reload the browser.
