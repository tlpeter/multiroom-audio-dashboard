# Multiroom Audio Dashboard 2.2.0-beta.6

**The equalizer follows your speakers.** The card now finds the amplifier controls of each selected speaker by itself, through the speaker's device in Home Assistant: the 15 EQ bands, EQ mode, channel mix, output trims, CPU temperature, WiFi, fault indicator, LED and fan. (#27)

- Speakers chosen with **Speakers beheren** get their equalizer without any YAML.
- After renaming a speaker (and its entities) the card picks up the new names automatically; old, no longer existing entity IDs in the card configuration are replaced.
- Entity IDs you configured yourself still take priority as long as they exist. Only entities of the same device are used. Set `auto_entities: false` to turn discovery off.
- Discovery uses the entity registry data Home Assistant already passes to the card; nothing extra is requested and nothing is sent to the speakers on load.

Tested automatically with a synthetic registry (unit and browser tests). Not yet verified on a live Home Assistant with renamed Louder-ESP32 devices.

Update through the Home Assistant Marketplace (formerly HACS) and reload the browser.
